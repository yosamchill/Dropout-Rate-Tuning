🔬 Interactive Dropout Visualizer

Slide to see neurons "drop out" at each forward pass! ✎

During training, Dropout temporarily disables random neurons with probability p. This forces remaining nodes to learn robust, independent features rather than relying on brittle co-adaptations.

(Balanced Regularization)
INPUT (Embed 32-d) DENSE 1 (Hidden 64) DROPOUT 1 DENSE 2 (Hidden 64) DROPOUT 2 OUTPUT (Sigmoid)

📌 Rate p = 0.1 (Too Low)

Minimal regularizing force. Networks can still overfit early and memorize training sentence tokens.

📌 Rate p = 0.5 (Optimal Zone)

The classic Hinton standard. Maximizes network ensemble variance and resulted in our peak test accuracy of 87.52%.

📌 Rate p = 0.7 (High Penalty)

Keeps validation loss lowest (0.2939) by severely curbing overfitting, but slows convergence.
